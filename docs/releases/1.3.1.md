Breeze 1.3.1 is the first release in this repository: a macOS menu bar app by Arika Labs for Midea air conditioners.

**Install:** download `Breeze.dmg`, drag Breeze to Applications, then allow it once in
System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" (the app is not notarized). Full steps at https://breeze.arikalabs.com/download.

**Requirements:** macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon, a Midea air conditioner (type 0xAC, V3 LAN protocol) paired in the
MSmartHome app, and that account's login for the one-time setup.

**Highlights**
- Local LAN control: power, mode, 0.5 °C steps, fan, swing, Eco / AI ECOMASTER, Turbo / Flash, Sleep, Breeze Away / Breezeless, self clean
- Room temperature and humidity from the unit, live power draw and kWh counters
- Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents), `breeze://` URL scheme, `midea-cli`
- Several units, cooling suggestions when your Mac runs hot
- Travertine (light) and Basalt (dark) themes that follow the system appearance
- Polls every 5 minutes in the background (configurable), 15 s while open

**Since 1.3.0**
- The shipped executable is stripped of debug symbol entries and is smaller.
- Protocol test vectors for discovery, LAN packets and V3 framing come from the public sample packets in the msmart-ng test suite; handshake inputs are synthetic. The tests now cover a V2 discovery reply as well as V3.
- The zip no longer contains AppleDouble sidecar files, so the bundle verifies after a plain `unzip`.

Tested on one unit (Midea AI ECOMASTER / Prime Guard). Please open an issue with Settings → Copy diagnostics if yours behaves differently.
