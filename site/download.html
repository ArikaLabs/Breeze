Version 1.3.1 · 2.6 MB · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

Download Breeze

macOS will ask you to allow it the first time. Here is how.

Three steps, once.

  1. 1

    Drag it into Applications

    Open the disk image and drag Breeze into the Applications folder. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

  2. 2

    Allow it once

    macOS blocks apps it has not notarized, so the first launch stops with a warning. Click Done (macOS 15) or Cancel (macOS 14), then within the hour open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. On macOS 14 you can instead Control-click Breeze and choose Open. It happens once. In Terminal, this does the same:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Breeze.app
  3. 3

    Sign in once

    Pick your unit and sign in with the MSmartHome account it is paired with. Breeze fetches the unit's key, checks it against the unit, and forgets your password.

Then click the temperature beside the clock. Will it work with my unit?
